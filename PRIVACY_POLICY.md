# Privacy Policy for Poly Oracle

Effective date: March 4, 2026

Poly Oracle respects your privacy.

## Information We Collect
Poly Oracle does not collect, store, or share personal information for analytics or advertising.

The app does not require account registration and does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

## Data Entered in the App
Questions typed into Poly Oracle are processed locally on your device for app functionality and are not transmitted to our servers.

## Optional Online Scoreboard
Poly Oracle includes an optional online scoreboard. If you submit a score, the app may store the initials you enter, your score, level or gameplay result, and the submission time using Google Firebase Cloud Firestore.

This information is used only to display the scoreboard and improve the gameplay experience. Poly Oracle does not sell this information, use it for advertising tracking, or require users to create an account.

Please do not enter your full name, email address, phone number, or other personal information as scoreboard initials.

## Tracking
Poly Oracle does not track you across apps or websites.

## Third-Party Services
Poly Oracle does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, or data brokerage services.

## Children's Privacy
Poly Oracle does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy
If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
