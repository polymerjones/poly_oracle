# Android perf pass — 2026-10-27

Emulator only: no Android device was connected. Medium_Phone_API_36.1 (Android 16), `-gpu host`, so
rendering ran on the host M1 Max through ANGLE/Metal and not on SwiftShader. Build 10:09 → 10:19, debug
APK. Probe: `rAF` frame deltas over CDP (adb forward `webview_devtools_remote_<pid>`), 12 s per
scene, fresh page load per level, plus `dumpsys gfxinfo` and process RSS. Firing = synthetic random
taps every 200 ms.

## AVD config matters more than the game
The stock AVD had **1 CPU core / 2 GB RAM**. That ran 1–20 FPS: kswapd was busy, 1 GB was in swap, and
the WebView renderer was at 420 MB. Raising it to **4 cores / 4 GB** (`~/.android/avd/Medium_Phone.avd/config.ini`,
backup in the session scratchpad) gave the numbers below. Earlier "emulator FPS is meaningless"
results came from SwiftShader and/or this 1-core config.

## Results (4 cores / 4 GB)
| scene | FPS | p95 ms | frames >33 ms |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oracle idle | 54.6 | 33.4 | 2.3% |
| L1 / L3 / L5 / L9 / L11 / L14 idle | 59.3–60 | 16.7–16.8 | 0–0.3% |
| same levels, firing* | 49.7–55.2 | 33.3 | 2.4–8.5% |

*The firing drop was **not** caused by firing. A/B on L9:

| L9 | FPS | >33 ms | main-thread busy |
|---|---|---|---|
| idle, oracle bg video decoding | 53.2 | 4.3% | 26.6% |
| firing, oracle bg video decoding | 55.4 | 3.6% | 27.1% |
| idle, video paused | 60.0 | 0% | 19.7% |
| firing, video paused | 60.0 | 0% | 17.5% |

**Bug, fixed in 10:19:** `primeBackgroundMedia()` had its Oracle-video condition inverted. If the
first gesture happened in-game (Android/web only), the hidden Oracle bg kept decoding under gameplay.
With that fixed, gameplay is a flat 60 FPS through L14 on this config.

## Memory
App process ≈ 365 MB RSS. WebView renderer 510 → 670 MB, highest at L11. GPU process ≈ 200 MB.
That's about 1.1–1.2 GB in total. Fine for 6 GB+ phones; worth watching on 3–4 GB Android
devices (low-memory kill risk).

## UI cross-check (Android vs iOS)
- The cockpit HUD, console buttons, comm box, NET switch and level borders all match iOS.
- **Fixed:** "You Died. Progress Lost." overflowed the right edge. Android has no Arial Black/Impact,
  and the fallback face is wider: 462 px on a 411 px viewport. `createFuzzyText` now shrinks to fit (396 px).
- **Open (needs a device with a punch-hole camera):** Android hides the status bar, so `safe.top`
  is 0 and the canopy text sits about 4 px from the top edge. It could land under a camera cutout.

## Caveats
This was a host-GPU emulator on an M1 Max. Real Android GPUs (Adreno/Mali), thermals, and the
hardware video decoder all differ. Confirm on the Retroid.
