Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Most of Poly Oracle runs entirely on your device. Poly Oracle does not require an account and does not sell personal information.

Optional Rewarded Ads

The iOS and Android apps offer optional rewarded video ads (for example, to continue a run or unlock a bonus). Ads are only shown when you choose to watch one. They are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interaction data, and diagnostic data in order to serve ads, measure them, and prevent fraud.

On iOS, Poly Oracle requests non-personalized ads only and does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. Where required by law (for example, in the EEA and UK), Google's consent form is shown before any ad is requested. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

The web version of Poly Oracle does not show ads.

Game Center

On iOS, if you are signed in to Game Center, your high score may be submitted to the Polyverse leaderboard. Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Optional Online Scoreboard

Poly Oracle includes an optional online scoreboard. If you submit a score, the app may store the initials you enter, your score, level or gameplay result, and the submission time using Google Firebase Cloud Firestore.

This information is used only to display the scoreboard and improve the gameplay experience. Poly Oracle does not sell this information or use it for advertising.

Please do not enter your full name, email address, phone number, or other personal information as scoreboard initials.

Children

Poly Oracle is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
